How to get a card

A card takes about two weeks. It costs fifty dollars to the state. It comes down to a doctor's signature and one form. That is the whole shape of it.

Start here

In 2016 the people of Arkansas voted yes. That is how this started. Medicine, from a plant, for people who are sick. The state says you may have it if you carry a card. The card comes from the Department of Health. Not from a store. Not from us.

It costs fifty dollars. It takes about two weeks. There is a form, a doctor, and some waiting by the mailbox.

We grow the plant. We do not sell the card, and we get nothing if you apply. We wrote this down because people keep paying twice for the same mistakes, and there is no good reason for that.

Am I eligible

Three things about you, and one thing about your health. Your doctor has to write down one of the conditions on the state list. Not something near it. One of these.

What a card does not do

· {{ l }}

Somebody can carry it for you

Not everybody can drive to a store. Some people are too sick that week. Some people are ten years old. So the state lets one person do that work for somebody else. That person is called a caregiver, and they go on the registry too.

{{ c.num }} {{ c.title }} {{ c.body }}

Do it early, not late

A card lasts one year. It does not renew itself, and nobody calls to remind you. The day it expires you are back to square one at the counter, so put it on a calendar the day it arrives.

· {{ r }}

Coming in from out of state

Your card from home will not work at an Arkansas register. It is not the store being difficult. It is the law. But if you already hold a card somewhere else, Arkansas will sell you a short visitor card of your own.
