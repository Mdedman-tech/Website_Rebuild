Chapter 01

Start here

In 2016 the people of Arkansas voted yes. Medicine, from a plant, for people who are sick. The state says you may have it if you carry a card. The card comes from the Department of Health. Not from a store, and not from us.

It costs fifty dollars. It takes about two weeks. There is a form, a doctor, and some waiting by the mailbox. That is the whole shape of it.

We grow the plant. We do not sell the card, and we get nothing if you apply. We wrote this down because people keep paying twice for the same small mistakes, and there is no good reason for that.

Am I eligible

Three things about you, and one thing about your health. Your doctor has to write down one of the conditions on the state list. Not something close to it. One of these.

Chapter 05

Caregivers

Not everybody can drive to a store. Some people are too sick that week. Some people are ten years old. So the state lets one person do that work for somebody else. That person is called a caregiver, and they go on the registry too.

Chapter 06

Renew and visit

Do it early, not late

A card lasts one year. It does not renew itself, and nobody calls to remind you. The day it runs out you are stuck at the counter, so put it on a calendar the day it arrives.

· {{ r }}

Coming in from out of state

Your card from home will not work at an Arkansas register. It is not the store being difficult. It is the law. But if you already hold a card somewhere else, Arkansas will sell you a short visitor card of your own.
