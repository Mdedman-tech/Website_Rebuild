# Project conventions: naturalstatemeds.com rebuild

Baseline for structure, voice, and craft: the **NSM Education Guide**
(project `7b05f252-54c7-4d01-85a0-f93fa5c15085`, `_stage/site/desktop.html` +
`mobile.html`). Match its level of finish. When in doubt, go read it.

## Typography (binding)
- Body / reading copy: **Work Sans** (`var(--font-sans)`). NOT Esther.
- Display & headings: **Burford** (`var(--font-display)`), sentence case,
  line-height ~1.0 to 1.14, letter-spacing -0.01em. Reserve all-caps for
  eyebrows and the wordmark.
- Eyebrows / labels / UI: uppercase, 600, letter-spacing 0.18em to 0.24em, bronze.

## Punctuation (binding, NO exceptions)
- NEVER use an em dash. Not in copy, not in headings, not anywhere.
- Use a comma, period, colon, or the word "to" (for ranges) instead.

## Voice
Patient-first, calm, plainspoken, specific. No SaaS copy, no stoner slang,
no emoji, no hollow claims. Say the real thing: numbers, days, temperatures,
names. "We love our plants, our people, and our patients."

## Palette
Arctic #F5F4E1 · Midnight #20253A · Bronze #B79550 · Cream #F6E7D7 · Haze #EFD7C5.
Never #FFF, never #000. Bronze is accent only.

## Compliance
Copy must satisfy Arkansas medical cannabis rules and be switchable to a
federal posture later. Disclaimers are data-driven, not hardcoded prose.
