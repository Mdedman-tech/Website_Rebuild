The same press as A, with the ink mottled inside the strokes. Best at large sizes. It disappears on small type, so it would go on the hero only.

The texture is two layers: a fine grain at roughly nine percent, and a much larger, softer mottling at five. On the landing it would sit under the topo lines, not replace them.
